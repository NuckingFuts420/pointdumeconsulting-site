Who can help set up AI for my business?

Look for someone who can tell you, before any demo, the one job the AI will do, which of your files it reads, who can see the answers, and who keeps it running afterward. If they can’t name the job in one sentence, they aren’t ready to build.

Four questions to ask before you sign

  • Which one job will it do, and how will we know it worked?
  • Which of our files does it read, and who can see which answers?
  • Can I see where each answer came from?
  • Who runs it after you leave, and what does it cost each month?

What a good first project looks like

One job that someone on your team does by hand every week and could explain in a paragraph: a weekly report built from three systems, a daily file that gets checked and published, a target list rebuilt each month.

Keep it small enough to check by hand for the first few weeks. A first project that needs a platform, a data warehouse and a change program is really three projects.

How we answer those four

  • One job per build, written down before work starts.
  • Your existing folder permissions decide who sees what.
  • Every answer and every flag carries its source.
  • The build is documented so your team can keep it running, and you can buy the code.
