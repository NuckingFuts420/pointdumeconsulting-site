How do I get AI help for my sales team?

Two things, in order. First, a target list built from public evidence, so reps call the accounts that are moving, with the evidence on every row. Then an engine that keeps watching those sources, drafts the research and the first message for each new signal, verifies the contact, and hands it to the right seller in the CRM. The seller decides what gets sent.

The list: who to call

Most target lists are built from industry, size and location. That tells you who could buy. It doesn't tell you who is moving. Public records say more: a job post that names the system a company is replacing, an enforcement action against a bank, a loan coming due on a building.

Each account should show how each signal is known: a record we read, a name we classified, or a proxy. Then a rep can tell a solid pick from an informed guess. Accounts nobody could check get marked that way, so they don't look empty.

The chore: one job, end to end

Pick a job someone does by hand every week and could explain in a paragraph. A weekly pipeline-change report is a good example. Then:

  1. Write the rule down, including the exceptions. If you can't write it down, it is not ready to automate.
  2. Let the software handle the clear cases from start to finish.
  3. Send a person only the exceptions, with the reason each one was held.
  4. Log every run: the input, the output, who approved it, and when.

What is the usual mistake?

Buying a tool that does ten things when nobody owns any of them.
