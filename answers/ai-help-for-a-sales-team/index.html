How do I get AI help for my sales team?

Two things, in order. First a target list built from public evidence, so reps call the accounts that are moving and can see why. Then an engine that keeps watching those sources, drafts the research and the first message for each new signal, and hands it to the right seller. The seller decides what gets sent.

The list: who to call

Most target lists are built from industry, size and location. That tells you who could buy. Public records tell you who is moving: a job post naming the system a company is replacing, an enforcement action against a bank, a loan coming due on a building.

Each account should show how each signal is known, so a rep can tell a solid pick from an informed guess. Accounts nobody could check get marked that way.

The engine: what happens next

Once the rules exist, they can run on a schedule. For each new signal the engine writes a short research note and a draft message, checks the contact, and creates a task for the seller in the CRM or Slack. Sellers rate what they get, and the rules get tuned from that.

The usual mistake

Buying a tool that does ten things when nobody owns any of them.
