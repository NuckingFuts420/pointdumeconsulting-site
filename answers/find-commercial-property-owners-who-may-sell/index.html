How can brokers research potential sellers?

We start with the broker’s territory, property types, and experience of good opportunities. We turn that knowledge into criteria we can check against property records.

Ownership changes, debt, and filings can give a broker reasons to investigate. We keep the records behind each flag and distinguish evidence from assumptions.

The broker reviews the shortlist and shares what conversations reveal. That feedback helps refine the next screen. A filing alone does not establish that an owner wants to sell.
