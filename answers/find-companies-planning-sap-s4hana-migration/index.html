How do I find companies planning an SAP S/4HANA migration?

Read their job postings. A company planning a move off SAP ECC usually hires for it first, and the posting says so: an ERP term next to a change word like migrate, implement or replace. Collect postings from the hiring systems and career sites your accounts use, match the phrase, and keep the sentence and link.

Why now?

SAP's mainstream maintenance for ECC 6.0 ends at the end of 2027, with paid extended maintenance to the end of 2030. Every ECC customer has to decide to move, extend or replace, and most of them staff up before they do.

Where do the postings live?

On public applicant-tracking systems such as Greenhouse, Lever and Workday, and on companies' own career sites. Job boards repeat the same posts with less detail. Go to the source the company posts on, so the text and the date are the company's own.

What counts as a signal?

An ERP term within a few words of a change verb: "lead our S/4HANA migration", "support the implementation of a new ERP", "replace our legacy system". That describes a project.

A skills wish-list does not: "experience with SAP a plus" tells you what the company runs, not what it is changing. Exclude it, or the list fills with companies that simply run SAP.

What goes wrong?

  • Shared postings. A parent job board can post the same text under many companies. Compare the text, and count a shared posting once.
  • Competing systems. A post about moving to another ERP is a signal too, but a different one. Keep a list of the competing tools and label which system each post names.
  • Accounts you could not read. Mark them "not checked". If they are merged with "no signal", you drop accounts nobody looked at.

A hiring-signal screen for an ERP consulting partner: 1,479 accounts searched across five applicant-tracking systems and company career sites, each flag with the sentence and the link.
