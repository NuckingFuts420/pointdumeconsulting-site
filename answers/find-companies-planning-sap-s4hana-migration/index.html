How can we find signs of an ERP project?

We work with the sales team to define the projects and buyers it wants to reach. Then we look for evidence that matches those criteria.

Job postings can describe a migration, replacement, or implementation. We check the project language and employer rather than treating any request for ERP skills as a buying signal.

Each match keeps its source and relevant wording. The seller can judge the timing and use what happens in the conversation to refine the criteria.

A posting can describe work already underway. It does not confirm an open supplier decision.
