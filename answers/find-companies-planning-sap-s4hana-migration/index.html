How do I find companies planning an SAP S/4HANA migration?

Read their job postings. A company planning a move off SAP ECC usually hires for it first, and the posting says so: an ERP term next to a word like migrate, implement or replace. Collect postings from the hiring systems and career sites your accounts use, match the phrase, and keep the sentence and the link.

Why now

SAP’s mainstream maintenance for ECC 6.0 ends at the end of 2027 for systems on the latest enhancement packages, with paid extended maintenance through 2030. Every ECC customer has to move, extend or replace, and many hire for it first.

Where the postings live

On public applicant-tracking systems like Greenhouse, Lever and Workday, and on companies’ own career sites. Job boards repeat the same posts with less detail, so go to the source the company posts on.

What counts as a signal

An ERP term within a few words of a change verb: “lead our S/4HANA migration”, “support the implementation of a new ERP”, “replace our legacy system”. That describes a project.

A skills wish-list is something else. “Experience with SAP a plus” tells you what the company runs. Leave it out, or the list fills with companies that simply run SAP.

What goes wrong

  • Shared postings. A parent job board can post the same text under many companies. Compare the text and count a shared posting once.
  • Competing systems. A post about moving to another ERP is a signal too, but a different one. Label which system each post names.
  • Accounts you couldn’t read. Mark them “not checked”. Merge them with “no signal” and you drop accounts nobody looked at.
  • Timing. A posting can describe work that’s already underway. It’s a reason to call and ask.
