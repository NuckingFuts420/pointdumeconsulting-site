AI operating layer: your data in one place, and the work across your firm automated on top of it

We get a firm's data into one place, connect the systems it already runs on, like Microsoft 365, the ERP and the accounting platform, and give the team one window to ask questions and do their work. Then we automate the firm's processes department by department, starting with the one you pick.

For example

  • Fund and investment questions answered from one place, like how many shares of a company the firm holds across every fund
  • Administrator statements checked automatically instead of in a quarterly Excel workbook
  • Capital calls, distributions and valuations run as workflows a person reviews

How it works

  1. Data first. Every automation is just reading data, so the first job is getting it out of spreadsheets and scattered drives into one warehouse an AI can read cheaply and accurately.
  2. Connect what you already use. Email, Teams and SharePoint, the ERP, the accounting system and the other tools the team opens every day, with access limited by user, so people only see what they're cleared to see.
  3. One window. Your team asks questions and does the work from one place, inside the AI they already use. A finance question that comes in by email gets a drafted answer for a person to approve.
  4. Automate by department. We start with the department that matters most, often finance and accounting, and work through the rest. Your people move from doing the work to reviewing it.

No assessment deck

We don't open with a months-long assessment and a roadmap. We start from what you want done and build it. A short questionnaire on what each department does is enough to plan and price the first stretch of work.

Paid as a retainer

Most of this runs as a monthly retainer: a set number of hours with your team and your internal lead, plus the occasional one-off build. A new use case that comes up along the way gets folded into the work already underway instead of turning into a new project.

How every project is built

  • Each build starts from the client's market, deal size and buyer.
  • Every row says where it came from: a record we read, a name we classified, or a proxy.
  • If we couldn't read an account, it's marked "not checked". It never shows up as "no signal".
  • Before delivery, an audit re-checks our numbers and we cut any claim that fails.
  • No per-run or per-outcome pricing: you pay for the build and a flat monthly fee for support, and every run has a spending cap.
  • You can buy the code outright, so what we build becomes your company's own.
