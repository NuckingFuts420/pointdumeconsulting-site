Account targeting

Your sellers already know what a good account looks like. We turn that into rules, check every account in your market against them using public records, and hand back a ranked list with the evidence on each row.

What that looks like

  • Companies whose job posts name the software they’re about to replace
  • Buildings whose deed, lien and court history says the owner may sell
  • Practices ranked for a buyer by the rates insurers have agreed to pay them

How it works

  1. Pin down the target. We sit with your sales team and write down who you sell to and what a good deal looks like, in their words.
  2. Turn it into rules. Each thing your sellers look for becomes a rule we can check: a job post naming the system being replaced, a loan coming due, a new regulator. A rule stays marked as a hunch until the data backs it.
  3. Build the whole market. Every account that could buy, including the ones missing from your CRM.
  4. Check every account. We read the public records most teams never get to: filings, job postings, court and property records. Each signal says how we know it: a record we read, a name we classified, or a stand-in measure.
  5. Rank it. You get the list in tiers with the reason on every row, so a rep can see why an account is at the top before picking up the phone. Accounts we couldn’t read are marked “not checked”.

After the list

The same rules can keep running. An engine watches the sources, and when something changes on an account it writes a short research note and a draft message, checks the contact, and puts a task in front of the right seller in your CRM or Slack. Your sellers decide what gets sent.

If you want the outbound as well, we set that up: sending domains, warmed mailboxes, sequences written from the signals, and replies routed to your team.
