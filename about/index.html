About Point Dume Consulting

Point Dume Consulting is Alex Kashtan’s AI consultancy in Los Angeles. We build systems that take real work off a team.

Alex spent more than eight years selling SAP and ERP software to manufacturers, distributors, life sciences companies and consulting firms. An ERP project touches every part of a company, and the deal doesn’t close until you understand how the customer actually runs: where an order goes, who approves what, where the work piles up. Our builds start from the same place.

Alex scopes every project and leads every call.

How we work

  • We start from how your business runs, before we talk about tools.
  • Every result shows where it came from. If we couldn’t check something, it’s marked “not checked”.
  • We re-check our own numbers before you see them and cut what doesn’t hold.
  • Nothing goes to one of your customers without a person approving it.
  • You can buy the code outright.

How a project runs

  1. Before the first call, we read what’s public about your company and the people who’ll be on it.
  2. The same day, you get a recap: what we heard, what we owe you and what we need from you.
  3. Then a short questionnaire your team can answer without technical help. Only the people who own the facts get homework.
  4. About a day after it comes back, you get a plan with a fixed fee for each phase and the tests each phase has to pass.
  5. The first phase ends in a written document that’s yours to keep, whether or not you go ahead with the build.
