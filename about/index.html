About Point Dume Consulting

Point Dume Consulting is Alex Kashtan and Nick Sneider, in Los Angeles. Alex is Managing Partner. We help a sales team define who to sell to, find the signals in its own wins and losses, rank every account in the market by the odds they win it, and build the agents and private AI that run the work around it. Then we hand it over running.

Alex spent eight years selling SAP and ERP inside hundreds of companies across manufacturing, distribution, consulting, life sciences, healthcare and fintech. We scope every build from how the client's business runs.

Nick is the engineer. He builds the systems, including the signal engine we use to collect and score public records.

How every project is built

  • Each build starts from the client's market, deal size and buyer.
  • Every row says where it came from: a record we read, a name we classified, or a proxy.
  • If we couldn't read an account, it's marked "not checked". It never shows up as "no signal".
  • Before delivery, an audit re-checks our numbers and we cut any claim that fails.
  • No per-run or per-outcome pricing: you pay for the build and a flat monthly fee for support, and every run has a spending cap.
  • You can buy the code outright, so what we build becomes your company's own.
