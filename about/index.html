About Point Dume Consulting

Point Dume Consulting builds sales research tools, business automation, and systems that help teams use their own information.

Alex Kashtan’s background is in SAP and ERP sales. The work starts with understanding the business and the people who use the result.

We work with each client to understand how they sell, what makes a good customer, and what signals suggest a buying opportunity. We turn their team’s experience and judgment into clear criteria, measurable signals, and ranked prospects.

That helps sales teams focus on targets that fit, show signs of interest, and have a credible reason to buy. We use feedback from conversations and sales outcomes to refine the criteria. The goal is better-qualified conversations and a stronger chance of closing, not simply more outreach.

For other business work, we follow the same approach: understand the task, agree how to measure a useful result, and test it with the team.
