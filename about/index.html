About Point Dume Consulting

Point Dume Consulting is Alex Kashtan, Managing Partner, in Los Angeles. We help a sales team define who to sell to, find the signals in its own wins and losses, rank every account in the market by the odds they win it, and build the agents and private AI that run the work around it. Then we hand it over running.

Alex spent eight years selling SAP and ERP inside hundreds of companies across manufacturing, distribution, consulting, life sciences, healthcare and fintech. Selling ERP means learning how a business runs, order to cash, before you can sell it anything: who signs, what the approval chain looks like, which system breaks first, and what event makes a company finally replace it.

A scoring model or an agent needs that same knowledge. Targeting works when the signals match how deals really get won, and an agent works when it takes over the right step of the job and leaves the judgment calls to a person. We scope every build from how the client's business runs, not from the tool.

How every project is built

  • Each build starts from the client's market, deal size and buyer.
  • Every row says where it came from: a record we read, a name we classified, or a proxy.
  • If we couldn't read an account, it's marked "not checked". It never shows up as "no signal".
  • Before delivery, an audit re-checks our numbers and we cut any claim that fails.
  • No per-run or per-outcome pricing: you pay for the build and a flat monthly fee for support, and every run has a spending cap.
  • You can buy the code outright, so what we build becomes your company's own.
