About Point Dume Consulting

Point Dume Consulting is Alex Kashtan's AI consultancy in Los Angeles. We build AI systems that do real work inside a business: finding and scoring the accounts worth going after, running the repeating jobs that eat a team's week, and giving a firm a private way to ask questions of its own documents. Then we hand it over running.

Alex spent eight years selling SAP and ERP to manufacturers, distributors, consulting firms, and life sciences, healthcare and fintech companies. An ERP project touches every part of a company, from sales and finance to operations and the warehouse, and the deal doesn't close until you understand how the customer actually runs: where an order goes, who approves what, and where the work piles up. Our builds start from the same place. We learn how the work gets done before deciding what a machine should take over, and the judgment calls stay with your people.

How every project is built

  • Each build starts from the client's market, deal size and buyer.
  • Every row says where it came from: a record we read, a name we classified, or a proxy.
  • If we couldn't read an account, it's marked "not checked". It never shows up as "no signal".
  • Before delivery, an audit re-checks our numbers and we cut any claim that fails.
  • No per-run or per-outcome pricing: you pay for the build and a flat monthly fee for support, and every run has a spending cap.
  • You can buy the code outright, so what we build becomes your company's own.
