Acquisition-target scoring

M&A advisory practice · DELIVERED

Scores psychiatry practices for a buyer on the rates insurers have contracted to pay them, from price-transparency files, the federal provider registry and Medicare data. 1,334 practices scored 0–100, with reasons and red flags.

How it works

We scored 1,334 psychiatry practices from insurer price-transparency files, the federal provider registry and Medicare data: 12 million rows pulled, 111,318 kept.

We built the reimbursement percentile ourselves, per insurer, state and billing code. A practice whose rate rests on one doctor is discounted, and rates shared by 300+ clinicians are flagged as bulk.

We tested 23 public practice traits as shortcuts. None predicted a high rate, so the list ranks on observed rates and says so. Each practice gets a 0–100 score, a tier, and the reasons and red flags behind it.

Before the list was final, an adversarial audit recomputed 27 of our own claims. Two bugs were fixed and the claims that failed were withdrawn.
