Acquisition research

For an acquisition adviser, we ranked practices against its research criteria using public provider records and listed insurer rates.

We compared rates within relevant groups, checked how much evidence supported each result, and flagged cases that needed closer review.

Testing possible shortcuts showed that public practice traits did not reliably identify higher listed rates in this analysis. We kept the ranking tied to the evidence instead.

Listed rates are not proof of payments received. The result supported further research, not a prediction of an acquisition.
