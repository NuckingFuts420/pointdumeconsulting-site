M&A advisory practice

Acquisition-target scoring

An M&A adviser needed to rank psychiatry practices for a buyer.

Working out what to measure

A private practice doesn’t publish what it earns, so the first question was what public information could stand in for it. We tested 23 public traits of a practice as shortcuts for a high rate. None of them worked.

What did work was going to the rates themselves. Insurers publish price-transparency files with the rates they’ve contracted to pay each provider. The files are large and awkward to read, but they hold the real number.

What we built

We scored 1,334 practices using those files, the federal provider registry and Medicare data. Each practice got a score from 0 to 100, a tier, and the reasons and red flags behind it. The list ranks on the rates themselves and says so.

Before the list was final we re-ran 27 of our own claims from scratch. That found two bugs, which we fixed, and we dropped the claims that didn’t hold.
