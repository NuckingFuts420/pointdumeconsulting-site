Off-market seller screen

Commercial real estate brokerage · DELIVERED

Screens a market's property records (deeds, mortgages, liens, tax, court and pre-foreclosure filings) and grades the owners most likely to sell. 2.9 million records, 51,037 tax lots screened.

How it works

We loaded 2.9 million deed and mortgage records into one database and screened 51,037 tax lots against deed, mortgage, lien, tax, housing-court and pre-foreclosure records.

9,186 lots were ranked on signs an owner may sell: a foreclosure filing, an estate or trust on title, debt per square foot above the market, an acquisition loan coming due. Blanket mortgages are split across lots by square footage.

For the top grade we machine-read 217 deeds, which removed 15 lots where the trust on title was a bank or a court referee. Every row says whether its evidence is a read deed, a classified name or a proxy.
