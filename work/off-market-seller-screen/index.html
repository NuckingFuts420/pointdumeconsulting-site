Property research

For a commercial real estate brokerage, we turned its target property and ownership criteria into a research screen.

We joined property filings, checked ownership classifications, and ranked owners for further research. Each flag kept its evidence and the limits of what we could verify.

Reviewing the records corrected classifications that a name alone had suggested. The result gave brokers a clearer starting point for conversations, without claiming to know who would sell.
