ERP sales research

For an ERP consulting partner, we screened job postings for language about business-system changes.

We separated project descriptions from ordinary skills requirements and kept the sentence and source behind each match. Duplicate postings and accounts we could not check were marked separately.

The result gave sellers specific evidence to investigate. It did not establish a budget or an open supplier decision.
