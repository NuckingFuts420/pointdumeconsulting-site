ERP consulting partner

Hiring-signal screen

An ERP consulting partner wanted to know which of its accounts were about to replace or move an ERP system.

The idea

Companies usually hire for a project before they buy for it. So a job posting for someone to lead an ERP migration is an early, public sign of the project.

Getting the postings

There’s no single place to read a company’s job postings. We read five applicant-tracking systems directly, plus company career sites, and searched 1,479 accounts. We found postings to read at 734 of them. The rest are marked “not checked”, which is a different thing from “no signal”.

Deciding what counts

Plenty of postings mention an ERP system only as a skill. The rule we settled on: a hit is an ERP term within six words of a change verb like migrate, implement or replace. “SAP experience a plus” doesn’t count. Each hit keeps the sentence and the link, so a seller can read it before calling.

Comparing the text caught 506 postings that a parent job board had copied across 13 accounts.
