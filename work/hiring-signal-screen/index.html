ERP consulting partner · DELIVERED

Reads job postings across five applicant-tracking systems and company career sites and flags accounts whose posts describe replacing or moving an ERP system. Each flag carries the sentence and the link. 1,479 accounts searched.

How it works

We searched 1,479 target accounts for job postings across five public applicant-tracking systems and hosted career sites, and read postings at 734.

A hit is an ERP term within six words of a change verb, with the candidate wish-list excluded, checked against 100+ competing tools in about 28 categories. Each hit carries the sentence and the link.

A text hash caught 506 postings shared across 13 accounts through a parent job board. Accounts we could not read are marked not checked, never no signal.
