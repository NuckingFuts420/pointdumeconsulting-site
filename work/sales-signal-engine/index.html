Pharma marketing company · in build

Sales signal engine

A pharma marketing company wanted its sellers to hear about a reason to call a drug manufacturer within a day of it appearing, without anyone watching the news for it.

Working out what counts as a reason to call

The rules come from the client’s own sellers. We interview them about which events are worth a call and which aren’t, then replay past events through the engine before it runs on live ones.

The events are things like an upcoming FDA decision date, a drug losing exclusivity, a competitor’s approval in the same category, or trial results for a new use of an existing drug.

What it does

The engine watches public sources for those events and filters them to the manufacturers and brands the client cares about. For each one that passes, it writes a short research note, a talk track and a draft message, checks a contact against two sources, and creates a task in the client’s CRM for the right team lead.

Nothing is sent automatically. A seller reads the research and decides what to do with it.

A second piece drafts the research sections of the client’s account plans from the same sources. A field with no source is marked that way instead of filled in.

It runs in the client’s own cloud account. We don’t hold their keys.
