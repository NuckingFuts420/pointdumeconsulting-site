Point Dume Consulting

Point Dume Consulting is an AI consultancy in Los Angeles. We do four things: account targeting and scoring, programmatic agents, an AI operating layer across the firm, and private AI.

What we build

Account targeting and scoring

We work with your sales team to define who you should sell to, find what your best deals had in common, and then score every account in your market on those signals, so you know which ones you're most likely to win.

Programmatic agents

A programmatic agent is software that takes over one repeating job, runs it the same way every time, and sends a person only the exceptions.

AI operating layer

We get a firm's data into one place, connect the systems it already runs on, like Microsoft 365, the ERP and the accounting platform, and give the team one window to ask questions and do their work.

Private AI

Private AI puts your firm's documents and history in one private system your team can ask questions, with each person seeing only what they could already open.

How targeting and scoring works

  1. We work with your sales team to define your ideal customer and your real market.
  2. We read your own wins and losses to find what your best deals had in common.
  3. We map every account in the market and check each one for those signals in public records.
  4. You get them ranked by the odds you win, with the evidence on every row.

Your sales team already knows why it wins: the kind of company, the timing, the problem that made them buy. We turn those instincts into signals we can measure, look for them across every account in your market, and rank the accounts showing them by how likely you are to win them. Underneath is a signal architecture: the rules, the sources, the evidence, the scores and a watch that re-checks them. How it works →

What we've run

Most of the work is data engineering. Some of the jobs behind the cases below:

  • 2.9 million deed and mortgage records loaded into one database and screened across 51,037 tax lots.
  • 12 million rows of insurer price files, provider registry and Medicare data pulled to score 1,334 psychiatry practices on the rates insurers have contracted to pay them.
  • Job postings read at 734 companies across five hiring systems. Comparing the text caught 506 postings a parent job board had copied across 13 accounts.
  • 217 deeds machine-read to check who really holds title, which took 15 lots off the top of a list.
  • An audit that recomputed 27 of our own claims before delivery. Two bugs were fixed and the claims that failed were cut.

Agents built to run cheap, for years

Most agent companies charge per run or per outcome, so the bill grows every time the agent works. We don't charge per use. You pay for the build, then a flat monthly fee for fixes, changes and support. The agent gets cheaper per job the longer it runs: most steps are ordinary code, a model is only called where a step needs judgment, like flagging a number that looks wrong, and every run has a spending cap. What it costs to run is hosting and those few model calls, not a fee to us.

Each agent we build has a documented interface, so the AI your company runs, now or later, can call it the way a person would hand a job to a colleague. Your AI brain asks for the weekly report or the checked file, and the agent does it the same way it always has, with the same checks and the same log. When you change AI vendors, the agents stay.

We'll sell you the code outright instead of licensing it to you. You get the source, the documentation and the right to change it, and the agent becomes part of how your business runs, on your books, not ours. Licensing is there too if you'd rather we keep running it.

How every project is built

  • Each build starts from the client's market, deal size and buyer.
  • Every row says where it came from: a record we read, a name we classified, or a proxy.
  • If we couldn't read an account, it's marked "not checked". It never shows up as "no signal".
  • Before delivery, an audit re-checks our numbers and we cut any claim that fails.
  • No per-run or per-outcome pricing: you pay for the build and a flat monthly fee for support, and every run has a spending cap.
  • You can buy the code outright, so what we build becomes your company's own.
